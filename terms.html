2. What Versa does

Versa measures your internet connection (download, upload, ping, jitter, packet loss and loaded latency), estimates how suitable it is for different uses, compares networks, checks the status of online services and offers network tools (diagnosis, ping, DNS, WHOIS, IP lookup and port checks).
