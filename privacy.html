Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 21, 2026

In short

  • No account. We never ask for your name, email or phone number.
  • Your tests, history, comparisons and settings are stored only on your device. We have no servers with user data.
  • To measure your connection, the app talks to speed test servers and to the services you test. Like any connection, they see your IP address.
  • On iPhone, iPad and Android the free version shows Google AdMob ads. The Mac version has no ads.
  • Purchases are handled by Apple or Google. We never see your payment details.

3. What we don't do

  • We don't sell your personal data.
  • We don't ask for your location, contacts, photos, camera or microphone.
  • We don't build profiles or send your results to our own servers.
  • Ads never load while a test is running, so they don't use your bandwidth or affect results.

4. Ads and consent

On iPhone, iPad and Android, the free version shows Google AdMob ads: an ad when opening the app, banners on some screens and optional rewarded videos that unlock the pro tools for 24 hours. Google may use cookies or device identifiers to show and measure ads. See How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services.

European Union, United Kingdom and Switzerland

The first time you open the app you will see a Google consent form compatible with the IAB TCF framework. You can accept, refuse or choose purposes. If you refuse, ads will not be personalized. You can change your choice at any time in Settings › Ad privacy.

iPhone and iPad

Versa asks for permission through App Tracking Transparency before accessing the advertising identifier (IDFA). If you don't allow it, the app works exactly the same and ads are less personalized.

United States

If you live in a state with a privacy law (for example California), you will see an option to opt out of the sale or sharing of your data for advertising.

How to limit personalized ads

  • Android: Settings › Google › Ads › Delete advertising ID.
  • iPhone and iPad: Settings › Privacy & Security › Tracking, and Apple Advertising.
  • You can also remove all ads with the one-time "Remove ads" purchase.

5. Purchases

"Remove ads" (on Mac, "Versa Pro") is a one-time purchase paid through the App Store or Google Play, which process the payment under their own privacy policies. We only receive confirmation that the purchase is valid. We never receive your card number or bank details.

6. Children

Versa is not directed to children under 13 and we don't knowingly collect personal data from them. If you are a parent or guardian and believe your child gave us personal information, write to us and we will delete it.

7. Retention and deletion

  • Results, history and settings stay on your device until you delete them in the app or uninstall it.
  • Ad and purchase data are kept by Google and Apple under their own policies. You can manage them from your Google or Apple account.

8. Your rights

You can request access, rectification, cancellation or opposition (ARCO rights under Mexico's Federal Law on the Protection of Personal Data Held by Private Parties) and withdraw your consent. If you live in the European Union or the United Kingdom you also have GDPR rights, and in California the CCPA/CPRA rights.

Since we don't keep user data on our own servers, the fastest way is usually to delete the app's data or use Google's and Apple's privacy tools. If you need anything else, write to luis.caballero1199@outlook.com with the subject "Privacy". We reply within 20 business days.

9. Security and transfers

Versa's communication with Apple, Google, AdMob and Cloudflare is encrypted (HTTPS/TLS). The free IP lookup service ip-api.com is queried over HTTP and only receives your IP address. These providers may process data on servers outside Mexico, mainly in the United States, with the safeguards required by law.

10. Changes

If we change this policy, we will update the date above and, for important changes, let you know in the app or on the store listing.
