Support

Something not working, or have an idea? Write to soporte.speedtestversa@outlook.com. Tell us your device, system version and what you were doing. We usually reply within 2 business days.

Frequently asked questions

Why is my result lower than what I pay for?

Wi-Fi is usually the bottleneck: distance to the router, walls, the 2.4 GHz band and other devices using the network all lower the result. Test close to the router on the 5 GHz or 6 GHz band, or with a cable on Mac, to see your real line speed.

What is "loaded ping" (bufferbloat)?

It's your ping while the connection is busy downloading or uploading. If it rises a lot, you'll feel lag in games and calls whenever someone else is using the internet. Routers with SQM or QoS help reduce it.

How much data does a test use?

Between 50 and 500 MB, depending on your speed. Faster connections move more data in the same time.

Which server does Versa use?

Cloudflare's closest data center, chosen automatically, with community LibreSpeed servers as a backup. Gaming ping is measured against the cloud regions closest to you.

How do I compare two networks?

Go to Compare, test the first network, switch to the other Wi-Fi or to mobile data and test again. Versa shows which one wins overall and for each use.

I bought "Remove ads". How do I get it on another device?

Open Settings in Versa and tap "Restore purchases", signed in with the same Apple ID or Google account. On Apple devices the same purchase also unlocks Versa Pro on Mac.

How do I delete my history?

In History, use the trash button. Uninstalling the app also deletes all its data, since everything is stored only on your device.

A service shows as down but it works for me.

"Is it down?" combines the official status page with a test from your network. If they disagree, Versa tells you. Check again in a few minutes.
